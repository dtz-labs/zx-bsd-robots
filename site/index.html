ZX BSD ROBOTS

The classic BSD terminal chase, rebuilt for the ZX Spectrum. Make the robots collide, keep clear of the junk heaps, and remember that teleporting is random—not safe.

Two machine-native editions. The standard game runs on a 48K Spectrum; the Timex build uses the SCLD’s real 512×192 hi-res mode.

Play in the browser

Choose an edition, press the play button, then click the display before using the keyboard.

Keyboard controls

Y K U / H . L / B J N
Eight-direction movement and safe standing—the original vi-style layout.
7 8 9 / 4 5 6 / 1 2 3
The same movement on a Spectrum-friendly numeric layout.
T / t
Teleport to a random empty square.
W / S / I / Q
Risky wait / safe stand / help / quit.
